## Your voice matters: Help shape your RMIT experience with SES

### What could make your RMIT experience better? From learning and campus facilities to student services, support, amenities and student life, your feedback can help RMIT understand what students need and where improvements can be made.

## Share your RMIT experience

The Student Experience Survey (SES) is your opportunity to share what is working well and what could be improved across your university experience.

Taking just 10-15 minutes to complete, the survey gives RMIT valuable insights into what matters to students and helps identify opportunities to improve the student experience.

## Turning student feedback into action

Student feedback goes beyond survey results. Hearing directly from students helps RMIT better understand their experiences and make informed improvements.

Dr. Jung Woo Han, Interim Deputy Dean, Academic and Quality, shared how student feedback plays an important role in shaping the future RMIT experience.

“When you first arrived at RMIT, some of the things you valued most, whether orientation, learning resources, support services, or study spaces, were shaped by students who took the time to share their feedback through the Student Experience Survey. Now, it's your opportunity to do the same,” he said.

Whether you want to highlight a positive experience or suggest what could be better, make your feedback count with the 3Bs: Be constructive, Be professional and Be open. Honest feedback is most useful when it is clear, respectful and constructive.

## Ready to have your say?

Complete the SES through your personalised survey link in your inbox with the subject "Student Experience Survey" from surveys@rmit.edu.au. You can also meet our student representative team on campus, complete the survey and receive an RMIT lanyard upon completion.

Your experience matters. Have your say and help shape a better RMIT experience.
